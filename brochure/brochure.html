Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What do you need at the venue?

A standard outlet, Wi-Fi, a table with two chairs and about 10 feet of open space in front of the mirror.

How long does setup take?

About 30 to 45 minutes. We arrive up to an hour before your start time, and setup never counts toward your hours.

Is there a travel fee?

Not inside the City of Boston. Outside the city, the first 20 round-trip miles are free, then $0.85 per mile.

Can you match our theme?

Yes. Tell us your theme and we will bring props to match. Send ideas and logos and we will design the print with you.

Are the photos printed right away?

Yes. Each photo prints in seconds as a high-quality 4x6, and guests can text or email it to themselves.

How far ahead should I book?

At least 30 days before your event is best. We will always try to fit in later bookings if the date is open.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A $250 non-refundable deposit holds your date, and the balance is due 14 days before the event.

Do you have a referral program?

Yes. Refer a friend and we will pay you $50 for every event they book, once their deposit is paid.
