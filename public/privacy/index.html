Privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026.

Magic Mirror Rental Services provides photo booth rentals in the Greater Boston Area, Massachusetts. This page explains what information we collect through this website and our events, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions go to inquiries@magicmirrorrentalservices.com.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, mobile phone number, event date, event type and message.
  • Bookings: when you book on our booking page, we collect your name, email, mobile phone number and billing address, your event details (date, times, event name and type, venue name and address, guest count and setup notes), your on-site contact, your package, print and add-on choices, your print template choices, any referral name you give us, and messages you send through your booking page.
  • Payments: deposits and balances are paid through Stripe. You enter your card details on Stripe’s secure checkout page, and we never see or store your full card number. Stripe tells us the amount paid and whether the payment went through.
  • Contract signatures: when you sign your contract online, we record your typed or drawn signature, your name and title, the date and time, your IP address and your browser type. These are kept with the signed PDF as proof of signature.
  • Emails: anything you send us by email.
  • Event photos: photos and videos guests take with the Magic Mirror, plus any phone number or email a guest enters to have photos sent to them.
  • Site visits: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without tracking you across other sites.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions, prepare quotes, take payments and plan your event.
  • To send you booking emails, such as your confirmation, invoice, signed contract and reminders about payments or details still needed.
  • To deliver event photos to guests and to you in your private online gallery.
  • To share selected prints in our website gallery and on social media. If you would like a photo removed, email us and we will take it down.
  • To keep the website working and understand which pages are useful.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the business:

  • Cloudflare: website hosting, the contact and signing forms, and analytics.
  • Google Workspace: our booking records (Google Sheets), our email (Gmail) and signed contracts (Google Drive).
  • Stripe: card payments.
  • Resend: delivering contact form messages to our inbox.
  • BoothGallery: online photo galleries.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries, booking records, payment records and signed contracts as long as we need them for your event and our business and tax records. Event galleries stay online after your event so you and your guests can come back to them. You can ask us to take down a gallery or delete your information at any time, except records we must keep by law.

Your choices

Email inquiries@magicmirrorrentalservices.com to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to remove a photo from our gallery or social media.

Booking terms

The terms for booking the Magic Mirror are on our terms page.

Accessibility

We want this website to work for everyone. If anything on it is hard to use, email us and we will help and fix it.
