Booking terms

Last updated November 23, 2026.

These are the terms for booking the Magic Mirror with Magic Mirror Rental Services. They summarize the contract you sign after you book. If anything here differs from your signed contract, the signed contract applies. Questions go to inquiries@magicmirrorrentalservices.com.

Deposit and payments

  • A $250 non-refundable reservation fee (deposit) is due when you sign your contract. It holds your date and counts toward your total.
  • The remaining balance is due 14 days before your event. If you book 14 days or less before your event, the full amount is due at booking.
  • Card payments go through Stripe. A $50 fee applies to any returned check.

Your service time

  • Your package hours run from the start and end times in your contract. If your event starts late or runs late, the service time stays the same unless you add time.
  • Extra time can be added at our listed rates ($75 for 30 minutes, $100 for 1 hour, $150 for 2 hours) and must be paid before the extra time begins.
  • We arrive at least an hour before your start time to set up and need about 30 minutes after the end to pack up. Setup and pack-up do not count toward your hours.
  • The booth may pause briefly for maintenance. We keep it running for at least 80% of your service time.

Date changes and cancellations

  • To change your date, email us at least 30 days before your original event date. The new date depends on availability and needs a new contract.
  • If the new date you want is not available, or you cancel for any reason, the $250 deposit is not refunded.

Refunds if we cannot provide the service

  • If we cannot attend your event because of something outside our control, such as severe weather, traffic, vehicle breakdown or illness, we will contact you or the venue as soon as possible and refund all payments you made.
  • If the booth runs for less than 80% of your service time because of a problem on our side, we refund a prorated amount for the time you did not receive.
  • Our responsibility is limited to refunding what you paid us.

Travel

Events inside the City of Boston have no travel fee. Events outside the city include a travel fee, which is shown in your total before you pay.

Venue, space and power

  • Please give us the venue’s name and full address, and make sure the venue knows we are coming and when we arrive.
  • The venue needs to allow access for loading and parking near the entrance. Let us know ahead of time about stairs, long walks from the loading area or other access limits. Parking fines caused by restricted loading are the client’s responsibility.
  • We need a 12 by 12 foot area, a standard 120 volt three-prong outlet, Wi-Fi, a table with two chairs and about 10 feet of open space in front of the booth.
  • Outdoor events need an overhead shelter that protects the booth from the weather. We may stop the booth if the weather puts guests, staff or equipment at risk. Payment is not reduced in that case.

Equipment

The client is responsible for damage to or loss of our equipment caused by misuse by the client or guests, or by theft or disaster at the event.

Photos

  • Your guests’ photos go to your private online gallery after the event.
  • Your contract includes a model release that lets us use event photos to show our work, such as in our website gallery and on social media. If you would like a photo removed, email us and we will take it down.
  • How we handle personal information is explained in our privacy policy.

Other terms

Your signed contract is the full agreement between you and Magic Mirror Rental Services and can only be changed in writing. If any part of it cannot be enforced, the rest still applies. Disagreements are settled by arbitration. We are not responsible for indirect or consequential losses.
