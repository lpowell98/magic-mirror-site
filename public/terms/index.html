Booking terms

Last updated November 23, 2026.

These terms govern bookings with Magic Mirror Rental Services (“we,” “us”) and summarize the Photo Booth Services Contract that each client (“you”) signs after booking. The contract refers to our photo booth by its equipment name, the Mirror Me Booth. Where these terms and your signed contract differ, the signed contract controls. Questions may be directed to inquiries@magicmirrorrentalservices.com.

1. Reservation fee and payment

  • A non-refundable reservation fee of $250 is due upon signing the contract. The fee secures your event date and is applied toward your total.
  • The remaining balance is due 14 days before the event. Bookings made 14 days or fewer before the event require payment in full at the time of booking.
  • Card payments are processed securely by Stripe. A fee of $50 applies to any returned check.

2. Service period

  • The service period runs from the start and end times stated in your contract. A late start or late finish to your event does not extend the service period unless additional time is purchased.
  • Additional time is available at our published rates of $75 for 30 minutes, $100 for 1 hour and $150 for 2 hours, payable before the additional time begins.
  • Our team arrives at least 60 minutes before the start time to set up and requires approximately 30 minutes after the end time to pack up. Setup and breakdown are not counted toward the service period.
  • We will keep the booth operational for at least 80% of the service period. Brief interruptions for maintenance may occur.

3. Date changes and cancellations

  • Requests to change the event date must be made in writing at least 30 days before the original event date. Date changes are subject to availability and require a new contract.
  • If the requested date is unavailable, or if the event is cancelled for any reason, the reservation fee is forfeited.

4. Refunds and limitation of liability

  • If circumstances beyond our control, such as severe weather, traffic delays, vehicle breakdown, illness or equipment failure, prevent us from attending your event, we will notify you or the venue as soon as possible and refund all payments received.
  • If, due to an issue on our part, the booth operates for less than 80% of the service period, you will receive a prorated refund for the service not provided.
  • Our total liability is limited to the amount you have paid us. We are not liable for indirect or consequential damages.

5. Travel

No travel fee applies to events within the City of Boston. For events elsewhere, the first 20 round-trip miles are included and each additional mile is charged at $0.85. Any travel fee is shown in your total before payment.

6. Venue, space and power

  • You are responsible for providing the venue’s name and full address, and for confirming with the venue that we will attend at the agreed time.
  • The venue must allow reasonable access for loading and suitable parking. Please notify us in advance of stairs, long distances from the loading area or other access restrictions. You are responsible for any parking fines resulting from restricted loading.
  • The booth requires a 12 by 12 foot operating area, a standard 120-volt three-prong outlet, a Wi-Fi connection, a table with two chairs and approximately 10 feet of open space in front of the booth.
  • Outdoor events require overhead shelter that protects the booth from the elements. We may suspend operation if weather conditions endanger guests, staff or equipment, and fees are not reduced in that event.

7. Equipment

You are responsible for any damage to or loss of our equipment caused by misuse by you or your guests, or by theft or disaster at the event.

8. Photographs

  • Photographs taken during your event are delivered to your private online gallery.
  • The contract includes a model release permitting us to use event photographs to showcase our work, including in our website gallery and on social media. We will remove any photograph on request.
  • Our handling of personal information is described in our privacy policy.

9. General

The signed contract constitutes the entire agreement between you and Magic Mirror Rental Services and may be amended only in writing signed by both parties. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect. Any dispute will be resolved by arbitration.
