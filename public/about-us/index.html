About our Boston photo booth company

Magic Mirror Rental Services started on the Northeastern University campus in 2019. Campus events are still a big part of what we do, and we’ve worked with student groups and schools at Northeastern, Boston University, Emmanuel College and Suffolk Law School.

Today we bring the Magic Mirror to weddings, birthdays, proms, galas and corporate events across Greater Boston. Weddings are our favorite. Every couple gets a print designed with their names and date, and guests go home with a 4x6 of the night.

Two trained attendants come to every event. We set up before your guests arrive and run the mirror all night, so you can enjoy the party.
