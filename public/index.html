Before you book

  • Where: Greater Boston, no travel fee inside the city
  • Setup: 30 to 45 minutes, we arrive up to an hour early
  • You provide: an outlet, Wi-Fi, a table, two chairs and about 10 feet of space
How far ahead should I book?

At least 30 days before your event is best, so we can hold your date and design your print.

Are the photos printed right away?

Yes. Every photo prints as soon as it is taken, and guests can also text or email photos to themselves.

Can the print match our theme?

Yes. Send ideas and logos ahead of time and we design the layout with you. We can also bring themed props.

Do attendants come with the booth?

Two trained attendants are at every event to run the booth and help your guests.

Is there a deposit?

A $250 non-refundable deposit holds your date. The balance is due 14 days before the event.
