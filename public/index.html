Before you book

Where do you work?

Greater Boston and surrounding areas. There is no travel fee inside the City of Boston.

What do you need at the venue?

An outlet, Wi-Fi, a table, two chairs and about 10 feet of space in front of the mirror.

How far ahead should I book?

At least 30 days before your event is best, so we can hold your date and design your print.

Is there a deposit?

A $250 non-refundable deposit holds your date. The balance is due 14 days before the event.
