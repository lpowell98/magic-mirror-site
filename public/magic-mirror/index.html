The Magic Mirror

The Magic Mirror photo booth is sure to be a hit at your next event. As guests walk up to the touch-screen mirror, colorful animations and voice guidance invite them to engage in a magical, interactive “selfie” experience. The Magic Mirror photo booth is completely customizable to match any event theme. The full-length mirror allows your guests to see themselves as their pictures are being taken. Our two attendants help every group find their pose, and our high-end premium props, themed to your event on request, keep guests coming back to the glass all night.

Animations

Ramp up the excitement with our built-in interactive animations. Each and every animation has been specially created to offer bright and colorful graphics that invite your guests to interact with the mirror. These animations are ready-to-use, tailored made for specific events, and offered in a variety of different languages.

Pose, Prop, Print

The mirror guides every guest with animations and a voice countdown, our attendants help with poses and props, and a DSLR with studio lighting captures every shot. Each session ends with a 4x6 print in seconds.

On-Glass Games

Our interactive mirror lets participants play games directly on the mirror. Combine multiplayer touch-based games and selfie photography at your next event for a mind-blowing experience.

The Magic Mirror includes four interactive games that you can choose to include in your event’s workflow. These games include: ‘3 Shell and a Pea’, ‘Tic-Tac-Toe’, ‘Pong’, & ‘Connect-4’.

Photo Retake Option

Every aspect of the Magic Mirror makes sure your guest walks away with a smile! With the Photo Retake feature, the guest has the option to retake a photo quickly that ensures their satisfaction.
