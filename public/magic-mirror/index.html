Magic Mirror

The Magic Mirror photo booth is sure to be a hit at your next event. As guests walk up to the touch-screen mirror, colorful animations and voice guidance invite them to engage in a magical, interactive “selfie” experience. The Magic Mirror photo booth is completely customizable to match any event theme. The full-length mirror allows your guests to see themselves as their pictures are being taken. The touchscreen interface provides them with the ability to edit the pictures using filters, signatures, and even emojis on the screen. For your more creative guests, all they need is their finger to make their own drawings and messages on the mirror. Coupled with our high-end premium props your guests will be entertained throughout your event.

Animations

Ramp up the excitement with our built-in interactive animations. Each and every animation has been specially created to offer bright and colorful graphics that invite your guests to interact with the mirror. These animations are ready-to-use, tailored made for specific events, and offered in a variety of different languages.

Photo Signing & Stamping

One of the most exciting features of the Magic Mirror Photo Booth is the optional, built-in Signing & Stamping Feature. Participants have the ability to insert emojis and draw directly on the mirror, which are all included on the final printouts.

The Signing & Stamping feature offers:

  • “Multi-Touch Support”, allowing up to 6 participants to get creative simultaneously
  • A selection of over 100 built-in emojis to stamp their images
  • Apply a vibrant neon color effect to their signatures
  • An “Undo” button that allows users to undo strokes an unlimited number of times

On-Glass Games

Our interactive mirror lets participants play games directly on the mirror. Combine multiplayer touch-based games and selfie photography at your next event for a mind-blowing experience.

The Magic Mirror includes four interactive games that you can choose to include in your event’s workflow. These games include: ‘3 Shell and a Pea’, ‘Tic-Tac-Toe’, ‘Pong’, & ‘Connect-4’.

Photo Retake Option

Every aspect of the Magic Mirror makes sure your guest walks away with a smile! With the Photo Retake feature, the guest has the option to retake a photo in a seamless, time-efficient manner that ensures their satisfaction.
