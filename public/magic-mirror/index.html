One session, start to print

Each session takes about a minute, and guests come back all night.

  1. Touch to start

    Built-in animations invite guests up to the glass. They can be tailored to your event and run in several languages.

  2. Pose and prop

    A voice countdown leads the shot. Our attendants help with poses and props, and a DSLR with studio lighting captures it.

  3. Retake if you like

    Guests can retake any shot before it prints.

  4. Print and share

    A 4x6 print in seconds, plus a copy by text or email, and the shot lands in your event’s digital gallery.
