What your event gets

  • A print design with your school, group or event name.
  • Unlimited sessions with every package, so everyone gets a turn.
  • Instant 4x6 prints, and students can text or email their photos to themselves.
  • Two attendants who run the booth and hand out the props.
  • A private online gallery of every photo after the event to share with your members.

Setup

We arrive up to an hour early. Setup takes 30 to 45 minutes and does not count toward your hours.

What we need

About 10 feet of space, an outlet, Wi-Fi, a table and two chairs.

Booking

A $250 deposit holds your date and the balance is due 14 days before. Book at least 30 days ahead when you can.
