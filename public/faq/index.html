Photo booth rental FAQ

Setup, travel, attendants, prints, deposits and referrals. Something else? Send us a message

QWhat do you need at the venue?
A standard outlet, Wi-Fi, a table with two chairs and about 10 feet of open space in front of the mirror. Guests need Wi-Fi to text and email their photos, and the open space gives them room to pose.
QHow long does setup take?
About 30 to 45 minutes. We arrive up to an hour before your start time, and setup never counts toward your hours. Please let the venue know when we’ll arrive so we can get in and test everything before guests show up.
QIs there a travel fee?
Not for events inside the City of Boston. Outside the city, the first 20 round-trip miles are free and each extra mile is $0.85, added to your total.
QWhich areas do you serve?
We serve Boston and Greater Boston, including Cambridge, Somerville, Brookline, Newton, Watertown, Waltham, Arlington, Belmont, Medford, Malden, Everett, Chelsea, Revere, Quincy, Milton, Dedham, Lexington, Winchester, Needham, Wellesley, Burlington, Woburn, Braintree and Framingham. We travel farther too; the travel fee above applies outside the city.
QDo attendants come with the booth?
Yes. Two trained attendants run the booth at every event and help your guests.
QCan you match our theme with props and the print?
Yes. Tell us your theme ahead of time and we’ll bring props to match. For the print, send your ideas and any logos and we’ll design the layout with you.
QHow far ahead should I book?
At least 30 days before your event is best. We’ll always try to fit in later bookings if the date is open.
QAre the photos printed right away?
Yes. Each photo prints in seconds as high-quality 4x6, and guests can also text or email photos to themselves.
QDo you have a referral program?
Yes. Refer a friend and we’ll pay you $50 for every event they book, once their deposit is paid.
QIs a deposit required?
Yes. A $250 non-refundable deposit holds your date, and the balance is due 14 days before the event.
