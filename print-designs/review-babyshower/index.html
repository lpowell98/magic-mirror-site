Baby shower print collection

Ten new designs for baby showers, gender reveals, sip-and-sees and christenings, each in 1, 2 and 3 photo versions. Every print is 4×6 at 300 dpi. The names and dates are placeholders: each family's own text replaces them. The photos are samples from past events so the layouts can be judged with real faces in them.

  • Pink, blue and neutral. Blush and rose for pink, powder and dusk for blue, oat, sage and butter for neutral. Each design is shown in one and can be switched to another.
  • For more than showers. Gender reveals (Airmail Reveal, Letter Beads), sip-and-sees (Muslin Swaddle, Nursery Mobile, Baby’s Breath) and christenings or baptisms (Christening Lace).
  • Real nursery objects, not clip art. A muslin swaddle, a letter bead bracelet, a gingham sheet with a sewn label, wooden blocks, a felt mobile, eyelet lace, a cross-stitch sampler, pressed baby’s breath and an airmail envelope, all drawn in code. No storks, rattles, onesies or cartoon animals.
  • Different from the other sets. Soft cloth, wood and paper in chalky pastels, with cocoa brown text instead of black. No gold foil.
  • One photo orientation. Every photo window is landscape, in every print and every version.
  • Photos come first. Wide windows with square or gently rounded corners, so a whole group fits. No arches.
