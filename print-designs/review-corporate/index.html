Corporate print collection

Ten new designs for conferences, galas, brand activations, company holiday parties, and restaurant, bar and university events, each in 1, 2 and 3 photo versions. Every print is 4×6 at 300 dpi and has an obvious spot for the client's logo. The event names, dates and the YOUR LOGO mark are placeholders. The photos are samples from past events so the layouts can be judged with real faces in them.

  • An obvious spot for the logo. Every design has a logo spot that suits the object: the badge header, the lobby wall, the VIP sign, the top of the menu, the plaque, the top paint chip. The previews show a sample YOUR LOGO mark there.
  • One version without the logo. Each print also comes as a transparent overlay with the logo spot left empty, so the client's own logo can be dropped in without redrawing anything.
  • Brand color swaps. Cobalt stands in for the client's brand color on the badge, step-and-repeat and paint chip. The rope, vermilion and copper can change too.
  • Real objects, not clip art. A lanyard badge, lobby letters on travertine, a press wall, a velvet rope, a leather folio, a brass plaque, a felt letter board, a dinner menu and a paint chip strip, all drawn in code.
  • Landscape windows, never arched. Every photo window is landscape and wide enough for a group, on both the landscape and the portrait prints.
  • For every kind of corporate event. Conferences, galas and award nights, brand activations and launches, company holiday parties, and restaurant, bar and university events.
