Wedding print collection

Ten new wedding designs for the photo booth, each in 1, 2 and 3 photo versions. Every print is 4×6 at 300 dpi. The names and date are a placeholder: each couple's own text replaces them. The photos are samples from past events so the layouts can be judged with real faces in them.

  • Two colors and gold. Each design uses one paper or ground color, one ink, and gold for accents.
  • Two typefaces at most. A display face for the names and Montserrat or Cormorant for the date.
  • Drawn, not clip art. Frames, laurels, the eucalyptus sprig and the deco fan are drawn line by line in code, so they stay sharp at any size.
  • Quiet texture. A light paper grain on every ground so prints don't look flat or digital.
  • One photo orientation. Every photo window is landscape, in every print and every version, so the booth camera stays set one way.
  • Photos come first. Windows are wide and close to the camera's shape so a whole group fits.
  • Same spot every time. The names sit in the same place in the 1, 2 and 3 photo versions of a design.
