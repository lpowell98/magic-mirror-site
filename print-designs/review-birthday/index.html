Birthday print collection

Ten new birthday designs for the photo booth, from first birthdays to milestone 50ths, each in 1, 2 and 3 photo versions. Every print is 4×6 at 300 dpi. The names, ages and dates are placeholders: each guest of honor's own text replaces them. The photos are samples from past events so the layouts can be judged with real faces in them.

  • Brand colors, used with restraint. Plum, orchid and gold do most of the work. Rose and honey appear only as small accents.
  • Fun comes from shape, not clutter. Confetti is spread evenly and kept off the photos and the name, so the print still reads at a glance.
  • Drawn, not clip art. Confetti, streamers, bunting, rainbow rings, marquee bulbs, champagne bubbles and terrazzo stones are all drawn in code. No balloons, cakes or cartoon art.
  • The age is the hero. Milestone designs make the number big and gold. Kids' designs say it in a sentence: "Leo is five!".
  • One photo orientation. Every photo window is landscape, in every print and every version, so the booth camera stays set one way.
  • Photos come first. Windows are wide, with square or gently rounded corners, so a whole group fits. No arches.
