Graduation print collection

Ten new graduation designs for the photo booth, covering high school, college and grad school, each in 1, 2 and 3 photo versions. Every print is 4×6 at 300 dpi. The name, school and date are placeholders: each graduate's own text replaces them. The photos are samples from past events so the layouts can be judged with real faces in them.

  • Every school level. High school (Chalk Notes, Varsity Letter, Composition Book, Graduate Stack), college (Felt Pennant, Gold Tassel, Diploma) and grad school (Embossed Seal, Library Card, Commencement).
  • School colors welcome. Blue, ivy and cardinal are stand-ins. Each design can be recolored to a school's own colors, while the gold, parchment and paper stay put.
  • Real objects, not clip art. A diploma with a foil seal, a chalkboard, a felt pennant, a tassel, an embossed seal, a chenille letter, a composition book, a library card and a commencement program, all drawn in code. No mortarboard icons or cartoon caps.
  • Different from the other sets. Academic materials (parchment, slate, wool felt, manila) instead of wedding ivory, birthday brights or holiday claret and spruce.
  • One photo orientation. Every photo window is landscape, in every print and every version.
  • Photos come first. Wide windows with square or gently rounded corners, so a whole group fits. No arches.
