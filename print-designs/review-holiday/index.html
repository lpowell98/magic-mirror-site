Holiday print collection

Ten new holiday designs for the photo booth, covering Christmas and winter, New Year's Eve, Halloween and general holiday parties, each in 1, 2 and 3 photo versions. Every print is 4×6 at 300 dpi. The names and dates are placeholders: each host's own text replaces them. The photos are samples from past events so the layouts can be judged with real faces in them.

  • A winter palette, not a costume palette. Claret, spruce and midnight carry every design, lit by gold or silver foil. Ember orange appears only in the two Halloween designs, and never as a background.
  • Every occasion covered. Christmas and winter (Papercut Pines, Evergreen Wreath, Nordic Knit, Frost), New Year's Eve (Fireworks, Midnight Clock), Halloween (Harvest Moon, Apothecary) and any holiday party (Candy Stripe, Gift Tag).
  • Drawn, not clip art. Pines, wreath needles, knit stitches, ice crystals, fireworks, the clock, the moon and its branch are all drawn in code. No Santas, pumpkins, ghosts or cartoon art.
  • Crafted materials. The designs borrow real objects: cut paper, a knitted sweater, a gift tag on twine, an apothecary label, so they feel made rather than generated.
  • One photo orientation. Every photo window is landscape, in every print and every version, so the booth camera stays set one way.
  • Photos come first. Windows are wide, with square or gently rounded corners, so a whole group fits. No arches.
